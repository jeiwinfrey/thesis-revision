The Model · technical notes

Pipeline, parameters, and evaluation

What the code in model_v4.py and evaluate_v4.py does, which numbers come from the literature and which are the study's own choices, and what the latest test run shows.

Pipeline

  1. Clean. Drop bad rows and duplicates; drop a fix when it implies speed above 50 m/s or acceleration beyond ±10 m/s²; drop fixes with error radius above 50 m when an accuracy column exists. A trip that loses over 20% of its records is set aside. Removed on the latest run: 0 of 2936 records.
  2. Measure. Speed over about 3 s, acceleration, jerk, turning, dwell time, route progress, distance from the route line, heading difference, rolling windows measured in elapsed seconds. GPS gaps break continuity and dwell.
  3. Context baseline. Median and spread of speed per 100 m stretch × direction × hour block, shrunk toward the stretch value (K = 8); typical dwell per stretch; a spatial index of earlier trips for distance from history. Fitted only on the history passed in, which is one commuter's earlier trips.
  4. Isolation Forest. Two forests, each fitted on half the history trips. Each history trip is scored by the forest that did not see it; a new trip gets the average of both. Scores are smoothed over 30 s.
  5. Concern level. Maximum of three levels: forest score as a robust z-score against the history, route-distance band (from the planned route and, once enough history exists, variants supported by at least two earlier trips), and current stop length as a robust z-score against locally comparable completed stops (cut at 2.5, 3, 3.5).
  6. Labels. The target is the worst concern level in the next 60 seconds when the full window has GPS coverage.
  7. XGBoost. Three cumulative binary models for level ≥ 1, ≥ 2, ≥ 3 (Frank & Hall, 2001). Recent trips are held out from fitting. Earlier held-out trips support Platt calibration and the last trip is used to estimate empirical prediction sets.
  8. Replay. Trips are judged one at a time in date order, each by a model fitted on earlier trips only, optionally limited to those within an expiry window.

Known limits

  • Generated trajectories exercise the pipeline. Independently reviewed field trips are needed to estimate alert performance.
  • Concern levels describe movement and are defined by the study rules.
  • Overlapping forecast windows are dependent. Prediction-set coverage is empirical.
